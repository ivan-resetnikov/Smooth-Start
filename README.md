> Automatic Minecraft Performance Installer

A tool that automatically sets up Minecraft performance mods and shaders, across game versions, mod loaders, and different launchers.

## The Problem 

On a weak machine, running even a fairly recent version of Minecraft can be a struggle. The whole experience feels slow, stuttery, and frustrating. My brothers have to deal with that every time they want to play.

There are ways to improve the game's performance, most notably through mods. But modding Minecraft can be a pain in the ass: finding compatible mods, installing the right loader, and dealing with the whole setup breaking whenever you change the game version or the mod loader.

I wanted to make it easier for my brothers to get a smooth gaming experience on their weak laptops without having to wrestle with all that setup.
